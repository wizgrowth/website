THE POINT OF SEO

Traffic is not the goal.
Being found at the right moment is.

People search because they need to solve something. Good SEO makes your business visible for those moments, gives them a useful answer, and removes friction between discovery and enquiry.

That means we do not report a keyword win in isolation. We connect technical health, search intent, content, local presence and authority to the commercial actions that matter.

HOW WE RUN IT

Less mystery.
More method.

You see what we see. The work starts with evidence, moves in priority order, and gets reviewed against business outcomes — not a pile of completed tasks.

01

Audit and baseline.

Technical crawl, Search Console review, analytics, keyword demand, competitors and current visibility. We establish where the site is actually starting.

02

Fix the foundations.

Indexing, site structure, internal links, speed, canonicals and structured data. Strong content performs better when the site underneath it is healthy.

03

Build for intent.

We map commercial and informational searches to the right pages, then improve or create content around what the searcher genuinely needs.

04

Build authority.

Useful digital PR, relevant citations, expert contributions and assets worth referencing. No link schemes or bought-link volume.

05

Review what moved.

Monthly reviews connect rankings and visibility to traffic, enquiries and conversion behaviour — including what did not move and what changes next.

BEFORE YOU ASK

Frequently asked questions.

How long does SEO take to show results?

Some technical and visibility changes can appear within weeks, while meaningful traffic and enquiry growth usually takes longer. The timeline depends on your starting point, competition, authority and the amount of work required. We establish the baseline before setting expectations.

Do you guarantee rankings?

No. No agency controls Google’s rankings. We agree the work, priorities, reporting and commercial measures in writing, then show what moved and what did not.

Do you do local SEO for Kochi and Kerala businesses?

Yes. Where local search matters, the work can include Google Business Profile optimisation, local landing pages, reviews, citations and location-specific search demand.

What does SEO cost at WizGrowth?

SEO is scoped as a monthly engagement based on the condition of the site, competition, content needs and the amount of implementation required. We confirm scope and price after the initial growth call and review.

Is SEO still important when people search with AI?

Yes. Search behaviour is expanding rather than disappearing. Strong technical foundations, clear entities, useful content and credible authority support visibility across traditional search and AI-assisted search experiences. AI Search Visibility is treated as a related but distinct service when deeper citation and answer visibility work is needed.
