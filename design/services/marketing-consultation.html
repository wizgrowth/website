BEFORE YOU ASK

Frequently asked questions.

Do you work with businesses outside Kerala?

Yes. Most of our work runs online, through calls, shared dashboards and written plans, so location doesn't matter. We're based in Kochi and meet clients in person in Kerala. Travel policy for larger engagements elsewhere in India is still to be confirmed.

What does the free consultation cover?

Thirty minutes on a call. We'll look at your numbers, tell you what we'd fix first, and follow up in writing. If we're not the right fit, we'll tell you.

What do you need from us to start?

View access to your analytics, ad accounts and, ideally, your CRM or sales data. We'll send a short checklist after the discovery call.

Can you review our current agency's work?

Yes, second-opinion audits are common. If their work is good, we'll say so. The audit's job is the truth, not a sales pitch.

Do we have to hire you to run the marketing afterwards?

No. The plan is yours to use however you like.

How is this different from your analytics service?

Analytics and reporting sets up the tracking and dashboards. Consultation uses that data to decide where your money should go. Many clients start with a consultation and add analytics if their tracking needs fixing.

What languages do you work in?

English is confirmed. Malayalam, Hindi and any other working languages should be confirmed before this page goes live.
