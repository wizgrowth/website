BEFORE YOU ASK

Frequently asked questions.

Which platforms do you manage?

Platform choice follows the audience and the job the channel needs to do. WizGrowth can support Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube, X and WhatsApp, with the mix scoped around the business.

How many posts do you publish?

Cadence is scoped around quality, platform and available proof. We would rather publish fewer pieces with a clear job than fill a calendar for volume.

Do you handle comments and DMs?

Yes, where community management is in scope. Response windows and escalation rules are agreed in advance so sales or sensitive conversations reach the right person quickly.
