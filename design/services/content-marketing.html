BEFORE YOU ASK

Frequently asked questions.

How often should a business publish?

As often as it can publish something genuinely useful. For many brands that means a smaller number of strong pieces plus planned updates to existing pages, rather than daily filler.

Do you use AI in content production?

AI can assist research, outlining and production, but the standard remains the same: useful expertise, accurate claims, real sources, human editing and a recognisable brand voice.

Can content support both SEO and AI search visibility?

Yes. Clear answers, strong evidence, useful structure and credible authorship can support traditional search visibility while also making a page easier for answer systems to understand and reference.
