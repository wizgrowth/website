BEFORE YOU ASK

Frequently asked questions.

How long does a website build take?

A focused service or brochure site can often ship in weeks rather than months, but the exact timeline depends on page count, content readiness, integrations and review cycles. The plan is agreed before build starts.

Do you build on WordPress?

Yes when a team needs that editing workflow. When a lighter static-first build is the better fit for speed, security and maintenance, we will recommend that instead.

Do you maintain websites after launch?

Yes. Ongoing care can cover updates, monitoring and small improvements, or the site can be fully handed over with training and documentation.
