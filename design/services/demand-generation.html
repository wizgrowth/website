HOW WE RUN IT
01

Tracking before spending.

We verify conversion events, attribution and the actions that actually matter to sales. Bad tracking makes every later decision noisier.

02

Funnel before budget.

Landing pages, message match and form friction are reviewed before spend scales. Better conversion often beats simply buying more traffic.

03

Structured channel tests.

Google, Meta and LinkedIn are used where they fit the buyer. Each test has a hypothesis, a threshold and a next action.

04

Creative without attachment.

Hooks and formats rotate because fatigue is normal. Weak ideas leave; strong ideas get variations.

05

Report against the business.

Cost per lead, lead quality, pipeline and revenue sit above reach and impressions. Misses stay visible too.

BEFORE YOU ASK

Frequently asked questions.

How quickly can paid campaigns produce useful data?

Campaigns can generate data quickly, but a stable cost per lead usually takes testing. We fix measurement first, then learn which audiences, messages and landing pages deserve more budget.

Who owns the ad accounts?

You do. WizGrowth works inside your accounts so the data, history and assets remain with your business.

Is there a minimum media budget?

The right budget depends on the market, sales value and channel. If the budget is too small to create a meaningful test, we will say so before taking on the work.
