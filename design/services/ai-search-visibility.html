THE POINT OF AI SEARCH VISIBILITY

You do not optimise a chatbot.
You strengthen the evidence it can find.

AI-assisted search experiences pull from many kinds of public information. Our job is to make your brand clearer, more useful and better supported across the places those systems may retrieve or reference.

GEO and AEO are useful industry labels. The practical work still depends on strong SEO foundations, clear entities, useful content, credible third-party signals and honest measurement.

BEFORE YOU ASK

Frequently asked questions.

What is AI Search Visibility?

AI Search Visibility is the work of making your brand easier for AI-assisted search systems to understand, retrieve and reference when people ask relevant questions. It includes entity clarity, useful answer-ready content, credible evidence, third-party authority and measurement.

Is AI Search Visibility the same as GEO or AEO?

They overlap. GEO and AEO are industry terms for improving visibility in generated and answer-style search experiences. We use AI Search Visibility as the clearer buyer-facing service name, while still using GEO and AEO where those terms help explain the discipline.

Can you guarantee my brand appears in ChatGPT, Gemini or Perplexity?

No. Independent AI systems control their own outputs. We can improve the public evidence, clarity and authority around your brand and track a fixed set of buyer questions over time, but we cannot guarantee a model response.

Do I need llms.txt or special AI schema?

No special AI-only markup is required for Google Search. Standard crawlability, accurate structured data, useful content and clear entity information remain the important foundations. We do not sell a technical file as a shortcut to AI visibility.

How do you measure AI visibility?

We track a fixed set of buyer-intent questions across relevant AI-assisted search tools, log brand mentions and citations where observable, compare competitor presence, and review AI-referred traffic and conversions where analytics exposes them.
